Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Introduction

FreeHongo is a free Japanese learning platform that allows users to take quizzes, practice writing and memorizing Japanese characters, view learning statistics, and create custom character lists.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, where it is stored, and what choices users have regarding their personal information.

FreeHongo aims to limit information collection to what is necessary to provide its features, manage user accounts, and protect the security of the service.

2. Information We Collect

The information collected depends on how the platform is used.

2.1. Use Without an Account

FreeHongo allows users to access its learning features without registering.

In this mode:

  • Learning results and related information are stored locally within the user's environment.
  • No personal account is created on FreeHongo's servers.
  • Cloud backup and synchronization of this information are not provided.
  • Removing locally stored information depends on the options available in the user's browser or device.

Local information may be lost if browser data is cleared, the user changes devices, or another environment is used without synchronization.

2.2. Account Information

When a user creates an account, FreeHongo may collect and store:

  • Username.
  • Email address.
  • Password hash, when using FreeHongo's own authentication system.
  • Internal user identifier.
  • Profile picture.
  • Authentication provider.
  • External provider account identifier, when applicable.
  • Account creation date.
  • Email verification date.
  • Last login date.

Passwords are not stored in plain text. Instead, a cryptographic representation is stored for credential verification.

2.3. Google Authentication

If a user chooses to register or sign in through Google, FreeHongo receives the information required by the authentication process, which may include:

  • Email address.
  • Name.
  • Profile picture.
  • Google account identifier.

This information is used to identify and manage the user's account within FreeHongo.

The use of Google OAuth is also subject to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

2.4. Learning Information

When an authenticated user uses the platform's learning features, FreeHongo may collect and store:

  • Custom character lists.
  • List visibility settings.
  • Quiz and examination results.
  • Learning statistics.
  • Information necessary to maintain learning history and progress.

This information is used to provide learning features, synchronization, and personal statistics.

Learning statistics are not published as part of a public user profile and are not used for personalized advertising.

2.5. Technical and Security Information

FreeHongo may temporarily process technical information necessary to protect the service, including IP addresses.

IP addresses are used exclusively for security mechanisms and request rate limiting.

They are not used to create advertising profiles or perform commercial tracking of users.

IP information is retained only for the technical period required by these mechanisms, generally a few minutes and up to approximately half an hour.

2.6. Tokens and Sessions

To maintain authenticated sessions and support account verification and recovery processes, FreeHongo manages session and temporary tokens.

These may include:

  • Session tokens.
  • Email verification tokens.
  • Password reset tokens.
  • Creation and expiration timestamps.
  • Information required to revoke or refresh sessions.

Tokens are stored using hashed representations and managed according to their respective purposes.

3. Purposes of Data Processing

Collected information is used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users through Google or their own credentials.
  • Verify email addresses.
  • Recover and reset passwords.
  • Maintain authenticated sessions.
  • Save and synchronize lists and learning results.
  • Display personal learning statistics.
  • Store and display profile pictures.
  • Allow access to and use of public character lists.
  • Maintain the integrity and operation of the platform.
  • Prevent abusive requests and protect the service.

FreeHongo does not use learning statistics for personalized advertising and does not sell users' personal information.

4. Information Visibility

FreeHongo distinguishes between private information and content that users choose to share.

Private information:

The following information is not part of a public user profile:

  • Email address.
  • Password or password hash.
  • Authentication tokens.
  • Personal learning statistics.
  • Quiz history and results.
  • Internal account information.

Profile Pictures:

Profile pictures may be publicly visible through the platform's features.

Character Lists:

Users can configure their lists as either public or private.

Public lists may be accessed by anyone. Private lists remain subject to their respective access restrictions.

FreeHongo does not publicly display the owner of user-created lists.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

FreeHongo uses a session cookie required to maintain user authentication.

This cookie uses security attributes such as:

  • HttpOnly Secure.
  • SameSite=Lax.

Its duration is associated with the validity period of the session.

FreeHongo does not use advertising cookies or its own analytical tracking cookies.

Additionally, the platform may use browser local storage to retain information required for learning features without an account.

Deleting cookies or local data may affect preferences, sessions, or learning information stored on the device.

6. Third-Party Services

FreeHongo uses external services to provide certain features.

6.1. Aiven

Aiven's PostgreSQL infrastructure is used to store account information, character lists, quiz results, and learning statistics.

6.2. Supabase Storage

Supabase Storage is used as an object storage service to store user profile pictures.

6.3. Google

Google provides the external authentication mechanism when a user chooses to sign in through Google.

Authentication may involve the processing of information by Google in accordance with its own policies.

6.4. Resend

Resend is used to send emails related to:

  • Email address verification.
  • Password recovery and reset.
  • Technical communications required for account management.

The use of these providers may involve processing certain information through infrastructure located outside Guatemala, depending on their services, configurations, and applicable policies.

FreeHongo does not sell personal information to these providers or use them to commercialize learning data.

7. Data Retention

Account-related information is retained while an account remains active and the information is necessary to provide the corresponding features.

Tokens are retained according to their expiration dates and revocation mechanisms.

IP addresses used for rate limiting are retained only for the technical period necessary for that purpose.

When a user deletes their account, FreeHongo deletes the personal information associated with that account.

However, certain character lists may remain stored under the generic attribution "[deleted user]" to preserve their functionality and allow other users who were already using them to continue doing so.

These retained lists should not contain personally identifiable information belonging to the former owner.

8. Information Security

FreeHongo implements technical measures intended to protect user information and prevent unauthorized access.

These measures include authentication mechanisms, hashed password and token storage, session cookie protections, and access controls for application resources.

However, no computer system can guarantee absolute security.

FreeHongo cannot guarantee that incidents, vulnerabilities, interruptions, or unauthorized access will never occur.

9. User Rights and Requests

Users may request information regarding their personal data and submit requests for:

  • Access to their data.
  • Correction of inaccurate information.
  • Updating their information.
  • Deletion of their personal information.
  • Information regarding how their data is processed.

Users may delete their accounts through the features available within the application.

For other privacy-related questions or requests, users may contact:

support@freehongo.edmondev.com

Requests will be evaluated and handled in accordance with applicable law and the platform's technical capabilities.

10. Children's Privacy

FreeHongo is a publicly accessible educational tool. Users must use the platform in accordance with the laws applicable to their age and legal capacity to accept the service's terms.

If a parent or legal guardian believes that a minor's personal information has been collected inappropriately, they may contact FreeHongo using the privacy contact address provided in this document.

11. Changes to This Policy

FreeHongo may update this Privacy Policy when new features are introduced, third-party providers change, or modifications to information processing practices become necessary.

The current version will be available on the platform and will include its last updated date.

Significant changes may be communicated through the mechanisms available within the application.
